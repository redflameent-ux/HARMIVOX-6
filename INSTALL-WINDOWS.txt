HARMIVOX-6 - installing on Windows
==================================

1. Copy the folder "HARMIVOX-6.vst3" into:
      C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
   (Open File Explorer, paste that path into the address bar, then drag the
   folder in. Windows may ask for permission - click Continue.)

2. Open FL Studio. Go to Options > Manage plugins, then click "Find plugins".
   HARMIVOX-6 shows up under Effects (made by RedFlame Ent).

3. Put HARMIVOX-6 on the mixer insert your vocal plays through.

4. In the plugin: Import page > Arm capture, then press play in FL.
   Stop FL when the section is done. Or just drag a WAV onto the plugin.

5. Tune page > Add voices... to build doubles and harmonies.

6. When you're happy, drag "Drag to playlist" (bottom left) onto FL's
   playlist at bar 1 to print the vocals as audio clips.

HARMIVOX-6.exe is a standalone version (no FL needed). Use it for the
Voice lab if routing a mic through FL is awkward: its audio settings are
under the Options button at the top of the window.

Your sessions, exports and voices are saved in:
   Documents\HARMIVOX-6
